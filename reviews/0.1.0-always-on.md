# Always-on review: dramatis 0.1.0, 1ca1b85...the release

**Verdict: GO.** No blockers. One major and six minors, all fixed before the tag, each with a test where one can say it.

Basis: the work after the nine-lens review (reviews/0.1.0-pre-minor.md, -dx.md, -quarterly.md): 8 commits, about 4,100 lines in src, site, tools and the Unity project. The three.js demo, the Unity Web player and its page, the eye art (fetched from the CDN, recoloured, mapped), the creator's save/rename popup and floor, the dev server's port, tunnel and rebuild-loop fix. Two lenses, the always-on tier (practices review.md), each an independent agent that tried to refute its own findings: correctness, and blast radius. Tier 0 (release-doctor) passes. Its one failure was the exact `tosijs-3d` pin excluding the newly published 0.12.0, fixed by moving the pin (every sample rebuilds equivalently on 0.12.0).

## Fixed before the tag

| Lens | Severity | Finding | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| blast radius | **major** | Every export fetched the eye art with no fallback: a `fetch` serving only the pack's URLs, or a missing eye file, made the whole export throw where it used to work. | A baked texture is an improvement, never a requirement: if the art can't be fetched or read, the eyes keep the plain off-white material. Network test; documented beside `fetch` (export.ts, export.md). |
| correctness | minor | The eye art was mirrored left to right in every export: the port kept tosijs-3d's `-x`, right for left-handed Babylon, wrong for glTF. | `x`, with the reason in the comment; a test pins it. The creator's preview (tosijs-3d's mapping, in Babylon) and the export now agree. |
| correctness, blast radius | minor | The creator cached a failed eye fetch for the session and never checked the response status. | Checks `ok`, forgets a failure, logs it, keeps tosijs-3d's eyes meanwhile. |
| correctness | minor | Moving tosijs-3d's iris dial repainted the shared eye canvas over the art. | The creator repaints on `iris` too. |
| correctness | minor | The demo and the Unity page let a slower earlier pick replace a later one. | The latest pick wins (a ticket per load). |
| correctness | minor | On /unity/ without a built player, loading a character waited forever. | Says the player isn't built and points at the three.js demo. |
| blast radius | minor | The PNG reader takes only 8-bit, non-interlaced RGB(A). | Not changed: the art is rendered by bin/eye-textures.ts (Chromium writes exactly that) and pinned by version; the network tier decodes the real files. Noted here. |
