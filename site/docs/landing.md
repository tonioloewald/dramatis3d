# Dramatis

<!--{"home": true, "pin": "top"}-->

**An open 3D character creator for the web.** Make a person, dress them, and
take them anywhere: CC0 base data, GLB and VRM out, and it works in any engine.

Dramatis is in development. The creator exists today as tosijs-3d's
[character builder](https://3d.tosijs.net/character-builder/); Dramatis is the
product built on it.

## What it is for

- **A character that leaves.** A character made here imports into three.js,
  Babylon, Unity, Godot and Unreal 5 with no manual fixes, and plays an
  animation you already have. That is the launch gate, and nothing ships before
  it passes.
- **Open data.** The bodies come from MakeHuman's CC0 data. Every asset states
  its licence on this site and carries it into the files you export.
- **Yours to keep.** The creator stores characters in your browser and shares
  them by link. There are no accounts.
